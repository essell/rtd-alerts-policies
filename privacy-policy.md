# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 7, 2026

This privacy policy describes how this personal, non-commercial project ("the Service") handles information.

## What This Service Does

This Service is a personal automation tool that checks public transit arrival data (from the Regional Transportation District, RTD, in Denver, Colorado) and sends that information via SMS text message to a small number of phone numbers chosen and controlled by the operator.

## Information We Collect

- **Phone numbers**: The Service stores the phone number(s) it sends messages to. These numbers are provided directly by the operator and are not collected from any public source or third party.
- **No other personal data is collected.** The Service does not collect names, emails, location data, or any other personal information from message recipients.
- **No data is collected from RTD or any third party about individual users.** The Service only retrieves public, aggregate transit schedule and arrival data, which contains no personal information.

## How Information Is Used

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver transit arrival text messages requested by the operator. No information is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than sending the intended message.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or any other information with third parties, except:
- **Twilio, Inc.**, which is used as the SMS delivery provider and necessarily processes the phone number and message content to deliver text messages, per [Twilio's Privacy Policy](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy).

## Data Retention

Phone numbers are retained only as long as needed to operate the Service and may be deleted at any time by the operator.

## Opting Out

Recipients may reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving further texts, or may contact the operator directly to be removed.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the operator directly.
