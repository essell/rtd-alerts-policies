RTD Bus Arrival Alerts

A small personal project, not a company or public service.

This project checks real-time bus arrival data from RTD Denver's public transit feed and sends a short text message to a couple of phones in my own household — mainly so my kid can glance at their phone and know exactly when to walk out the door.

Who runs this

It's operated by one person (me), for personal, non-commercial use. There's no company, no sign-up page, and no public list of recipients — I add phone numbers myself, one at a time, for people I know directly.

What the messages contain

Just transit arrival times and the cross streets of the stop, nothing else. No marketing, no third-party content, no data collection beyond the phone numbers needed to deliver the text.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving alerts, or HELP for assistance.

This page exists mainly to describe the project for messaging-provider verification purposes. If you have questions about it, you can reach the operator directly.
